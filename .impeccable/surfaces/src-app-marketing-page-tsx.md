# Landing page

Mode: Persuade. Public page at `/` for signed-out visitors (signed-in users go to /jobs). Linear: LAY-131.

Audience: any active job seeker, arriving from search or a shared link, often in the evening on a laptop or phone. Job: decide that LayMeOn is worth starting. Action: paste a job link and sign up. Proof: the product's own mechanism shown with labeled sample content (Documents → Career Story → Match Report → Tailoring → Interview Prep). Constraints: no prices, testimonials, customer names or usage numbers; name is "LayMeOn" for now and must swap easily.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The search as a visit to the pavilion of your own future: an early-80s world's fair at dusk where each stage of LayMeOn is a pavilion on one monorail loop, and the Career Story is the centerpiece. Refuses the split hero with a screenshot and a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: "Salvage Dusk" palette, chosen by the owner on 2026-11-26 to replace the original saturated set as too high-contrast and garish. Blue-black navy sky (#0F1626) as ground with a fine print grain, parchment (#ECE2D0) for cards and the light passage, ink (#1A1420) on parchment, an amber (#DE9E2E) button, and a four-line ribbon in sun-faded tones (rust #A8473A, amber #DE9E2E, sage #6F9A6A, weathered cyan #5B98A6) that is the monorail track. Background texture: topographic contours that peak in a hill under the hero guide map, tinted by height in the four line colors, then relax into even faint hairlines (a seamless survey tile) down the rest of the page. Both are static SVGs in public/marketing/, generated once. Headlines in Michroma (wide Eurostile lineage); text in Archivo. Wordmark letters cut by horizontal stripes. Rounded pavilion signage panels, isometric flat-toned buildings, round stop markers on the line. References for tone: Starfield and ARC Raiders sites (dusty, worn retro-futurism). No neon glow, chrome, synthwave grids, sunsets, or primary-strength color.

STORY: Visitors understand that LayMeOn builds everything from their own Documents, believe it never invents experience because they watch Evidence link a resume line to a Requirement, and paste a job link to start.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Signage-strip nav (striped wordmark, How it works, Log in, Get started). Left 5/12: Michroma headline, one-sentence promise, the "ticket booth" job-link field with its button. Right 7/12: an isometric guide map with five pavilions on the ribbon loop, labeled on signs, a monorail car circulating. Ribbon exits the map down into the tour.

FORM: Grounded candidate 1 of 7 (picked over the roll), seed:ecb2c649b856c850. Signature interaction: the tour, where the ribbon runs down the page and a car rides it with scroll, stopping at five pavilions, each with a sample demo.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
