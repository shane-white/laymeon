# LayMeOn

LayMeOn helps a job seeker keep their own career documents in one place, build a full account of their experience from them, and see how well a resume fits each job they're pursuing.

## Language

### Documents

**Document**:
A piece of the user's own writing that LayMeOn keeps: a Resume, a Cover letter or an Example Work.
_Avoid_: File, upload, attachment

**Resume**:
A Document that summarizes the user's experience for applications. A user can keep several, each aimed at a different Target role.
_Avoid_: CV, résumé

**Cover letter**:
A Document written to accompany an application.

**Example Work**:
A Document describing one project the user did, typed in or split out of a file the user uploaded.
_Avoid_: Project description, portfolio item

**Source**:
How a Document came to exist: uploaded from a file, written in the app, or generated by LayMeOn. Only uploaded and written Documents are the user's own account of their experience.

**Default document**:
The Resume or Cover letter used for a Job when the user hasn't picked one. Each type has at most one.
_Avoid_: Primary, main resume

**Target role**:
The kind of role a Document is aimed at, such as "Product Designer".

**Source material**:
The parts of a Document that say something about the user's experience: its text, an Example Work's title, and its Target role. Which Document is the Default document, and a Resume's or Cover letter's name, are not Source material.

**Document change**:
Adding, replacing, editing or deleting a Document. A Document change that touches Source material calls for a Rebuild.

**Replace**:
Swapping an uploaded Document's file for a new one while it keeps its name, Target role, Default document setting and Jobs.
_Avoid_: Re-upload, update

**Earlier version**:
A saved result (a Resume Review, Match Report, Tailoring or a Practice card's hint) made from a Document's Source material before it last changed, a Practice card's hint made from the Career Story before it last changed (a Rebuild or a User change), or a Tailoring made from a Match Report that has since been rerun.
_Avoid_: Stale, outdated

### Career Story

**Career Story**:
The full account of the user's experience, built from all their own Documents and corrected by their User changes.
_Avoid_: Profile, master resume

**Extraction**:
What Claude reads out of one Document for the Career Story: jobs, accomplishments, skills and education.

**Rebuild**:
Making the Career Story again from the current Documents, after a Document change that touches Source material.
_Avoid_: Refresh, regenerate

**User change**:
A correction the user makes to the Career Story (an edit, removal, addition or merge) that is kept and re-applied after every Rebuild.
_Avoid_: Override

**Skill synonyms**:
Skills named differently across Documents that mean the same thing, counted as one skill.

### Jobs

**Job**:
A job posting the user has saved and is tracking, with its status: interested, applied, interviewing or closed.
_Avoid_: Listing, application, posting

**Match Report**:
A structured comparison of one Resume against one Job, rated strong, partial or weak. Shown as "Resume Match Analysis".
_Avoid_: Job match, analysis

**Requirement**:
Something a Job asks for, either must-have or nice-to-have, and how well the Resume meets it: strong, partial or missing.

**Screener**:
A hard Requirement that filters applicants out on its own, such as years of experience, a degree, a license or certification, a security clearance, or a required location. A location Screener (on-site, in-office or region-limited) can't be met by changing the Resume, so the Tailoring leaves it out.

**Search words**:
The terms software that screens applications would search a Resume for.
_Avoid_: Keywords

**Evidence**:
A word-for-word quote from a Document that backs up a claim in a Match Report or the Career Story.

**Tailoring**:
Working one Resume against one Job's Match Report, one Suggestion at a time, to make a version of the Resume for that Job. Each Job and Resume pair has at most one.
_Avoid_: Revision, tailored resume

**Suggestion**:
A proposed edit to one Resume for one Job, aimed at one Requirement or Screener: either rewriting text already on the Resume or adding new text, backed by Evidence. The user accepts, edits or rejects each one.
_Avoid_: Recommendation, fix, change

**Decision**:
What the user chooses for one item in a Tailoring: Accept, Edit, Reject, Skip or I don't have this. A Decision can be undone.

**Not had**:
A Decision that the user lacks a missing Requirement or a Screener the Resume doesn't show, made with "I don't have this". It closes the item for this Tailoring, and nothing goes on the Resume.
_Avoid_: Rejected, skipped, N/A

**Analyses**:
The older free-text suggestions on a Job for the cover letter. Its free-text suggestions for the resume were retired: the Tailoring replaces them, and those for the interview were replaced by Interview Prep.

**Interview Prep**:
What LayMeOn gathers to help the user prepare for one Job's interviews: the company's interview process, a briefing on the company, and practice.
_Avoid_: Interview suggestions

**Practice card**:
A likely interview question in Interview Prep, with a hint on the back: what the interviewer is probing for, a rough outline of an answer, and which of the user's experiences fit. The user can mark it Feels good or Practice more; until then it's Not tried yet. Nothing is scored.
_Avoid_: Flashcard, quiz question

**Deck**:
A group of Practice cards on one theme, such as behavioral questions or questions to ask the interviewer.

**Practice session**:
Going through Practice cards one at a time, either one Deck or a Quick mix of a few cards from every Deck, marking each as you go. It ends with a count of the marks, never a score.

### Resume Review

**Resume Review**:
A slide deck that reviews one Resume on its own, combining checks made in code with Claude's review.

**Overall label**:
The Resume Review's verdict: Ready to send, A few fixes, or Needs work.

**Check label**:
The result of one check in a Resume Review: Fix this, Could improve, Good, or Info.

**Software view**:
A Resume's text as software that screens applications reads it, shown next to what Claude read.

**Read further**:
Sources a person has checked, linked from a Resume Review's checks for users who want more detail.
