# Instant payouts for Ledgerly customers

At Ledgerly, I led the launch of ACH and instant card payouts so small businesses could get paid on invoices the same day.

- Worked with our payment processor and two engineers to add ACH and debit-card payouts
- Partnered with our security team on the PCI DSS scope for storing card details
- Payout volume grew to $4M a month within two quarters
