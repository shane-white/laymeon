# Marketing Analyst — Brightpath Learning

Brightpath sells online courses to adult learners. Our marketing analyst helps the growth team decide where to spend.

## What you'll need

- 2+ years in a marketing analytics role
- Experience with Google Analytics 4
- Experience with search engine optimization
- Experience running A/B tests on landing pages or emails
- Experience with customer relationship management software such as Salesforce
- Experience building dashboards in Tableau or Looker

## Nice to have

- Python for data analysis
- Experience with paid social campaigns
